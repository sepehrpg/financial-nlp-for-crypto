SEC Charges International Dealer That Sold Security-Based Swaps to U.S. Investors

For Immediate Release

2019-226

Washington D.C., 2019-10-31 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission charged XBT Corp. SARL, doing business as First Global Credit, for offering and selling unregistered security-based swaps to U.S. investors using bitcoins and for failing to transact swaps on a registered national exchange.

The company targeted U.S. individuals to deposit and use bitcoins to buy and sell investment products, including bitcoin Asset Linked Notes, without satisfying registration and exchange requirements.

Without admitting or denying the findings, First Global Credit consented to a cease-and-desist order and agreed to pay disgorgement and a penalty.
