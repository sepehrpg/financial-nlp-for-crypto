SEC Charges Founder of $1.7 Billion HyperFund Crypto Pyramid Scheme and Top Promoter with Fraud

Promoter Brenda Chunga, also known as Bitcoin Beautee, agrees to settle fraud and unregistered offering charges

For Immediate Release

2024-11

Washington D.C., 2024-01-29 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission charged Xue Lee and Brenda Chunga, also known as Bitcoin Beautee, for their involvement in the HyperFund crypto asset pyramid scheme that raised more than $1.7 billion from investors worldwide.

From June 2020 through early 2022, the defendants promoted membership packages that allegedly guaranteed high returns from supposed crypto asset mining operations.

The SEC alleges HyperFund had no real source of revenue other than investor funds and collapsed in 2022 when investors could no longer make withdrawals.

The complaint seeks injunctive relief, disgorgement, prejudgment interest, and civil penalties. Chunga agreed to settle, while the charges against Lee were to be litigated.
