eToro Reaches Settlement with SEC and Will Cease Trading Activity in Nearly All Crypto Assets

Firm charged with operating unregistered broker, clearing agency in connection with its crypto asset trading platform

For Immediate Release

2024-125

Washington D.C., 2024-09-12 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission announced that eToro USA LLC agreed to pay $1.5 million to settle charges that it operated an unregistered broker and clearing agency in connection with a platform that facilitated buying and selling certain crypto assets as securities.

The SEC order finds that, since at least 2020, eToro provided U.S. customers the ability to trade crypto assets being offered and sold as securities without complying with registration provisions.

eToro announced that the only crypto assets U.S. customers could continue to trade would be Bitcoin, Bitcoin Cash, and Ether. Customers would have 180 days to sell other crypto assets after the order.

Without admitting or denying the findings, eToro agreed to a cease-and-desist order, the penalty, and a process to liquidate assets it could not transfer to customers and return proceeds.
