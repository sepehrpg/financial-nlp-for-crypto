SEC Halts Fraudulent Cryptomining and Trading Scheme

Founders allegedly promised big returns, instead used investments to fund lavish lifestyles

For Immediate Release

2022-81

Washington D.C., 2022-05-06 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission announced fraud charges against Mining Capital Coin Corp., its founders, and related entities in connection with unregistered offerings and fraudulent sales of mining packages to thousands of investors.

The complaint alleges that 65,535 investors were promised daily returns from cryptocurrency mining, stock and foreign exchange trading, and cryptocurrency arbitrage.

In the early days, investors were promised returns in Bitcoin, but later were required to withdraw in Capital Coin, the company’s own token, using a fake trading platform.

The complaint seeks injunctions, disgorgement, civil penalties, and officer and director bars.
