SEC Files Fraud Charges in Bitcoin and Office Space Investment Schemes

For Immediate Release

2017-123

Washington D.C., 2017-06-30 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission filed fraud charges against the founder of a purported Bitcoin platform and a chain of co-working spaces, alleging that investors were misled in both companies.

The SEC alleges sales representatives sold securities in Bitcoin Store Inc. and Bar Works Inc. using offering materials that misrepresented executives and company operations.

Materials claimed Bitcoin Store was an easy-to-use and secure way of holding and trading Bitcoin and had generated millions in sales. The SEC alleges the company had no operations and did not generate the claimed revenue.

The SEC obtained an emergency asset freeze and alleged violations of antifraud and registration provisions.
