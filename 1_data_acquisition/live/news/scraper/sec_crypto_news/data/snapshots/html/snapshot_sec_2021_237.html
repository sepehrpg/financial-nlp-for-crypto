SEC Charges Promoter with Conducting Cryptocurrency Investment Scams

For Immediate Release

2021-237

Washington D.C., 2021-11-18 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission charged Ryan Ginster with conducting two unregistered and fraudulent securities offerings that raised more than $3.6 million in cryptocurrency from retail investors.

The complaint alleges that from 2018 to 2021 Ginster raised approximately $3.6 million in Bitcoin through two online platforms that promised astronomical rates of return through cryptocurrency trading and advertising arbitrage.

The SEC alleges Ginster misappropriated at least $1 million for personal expenses. The complaint seeks permanent injunctions, disgorgement with prejudgment interest, and civil penalties.
