SEC Charges Bitcoin Mining Companies

For Immediate Release

2015-271

Washington D.C., 2015-12-01 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission charged two Bitcoin mining companies and their founder with conducting a Ponzi scheme that used the lure of virtual-currency riches to defraud investors.

The complaint alleges GAW Miners and ZenMiner purported to offer shares of a digital Bitcoin mining operation but did not own enough computing power to conduct the promised mining.

More than 10,000 investors purchased Hashlets. Investors were allegedly misled to believe they would share in returns from Bitcoin mining, while returns paid to some investors came from proceeds collected from others.

The complaint seeks permanent injunctive relief, disgorgement, prejudgment interest, and penalties.
