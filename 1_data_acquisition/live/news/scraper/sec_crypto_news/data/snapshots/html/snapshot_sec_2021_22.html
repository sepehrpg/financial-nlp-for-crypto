SEC Charges Three Individuals in Digital Asset Frauds

For Immediate Release

2021-22

Washington D.C., 2021-02-01 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission charged three individuals with defrauding hundreds of retail investors out of more than $11 million through fraudulent and unregistered digital asset securities offerings.

The complaint alleges the defendants promoted Start Options and Bitcoiin2Gen. They falsely claimed Start Options was the largest Bitcoin exchange in euro volume and liquidity and was consistently rated the best and most secure Bitcoin exchange.

Promotional materials also allegedly made false claims about B2G tokens and the use of investor funds. The complaint seeks injunctive relief, disgorgement plus interest, penalties, and officer-and-director bars.
