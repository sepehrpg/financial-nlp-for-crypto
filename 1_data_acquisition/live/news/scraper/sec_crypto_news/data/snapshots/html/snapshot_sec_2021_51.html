SEC Charges California-Based Fraudster With Selling Insider Tips on the Dark Web

For Immediate Release

2021-51

Washington D.C., 2021-03-18 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission charged James Roland Jones with a fraudulent scheme to sell purported insider tips on the dark web, the SEC’s first enforcement action involving alleged securities violations on the dark web.

The complaint alleges Jones offered and sold purported insider tips that he falsely described as material, nonpublic information. Several users paying in bitcoin purchased the tips and traded based on the information.

Jones agreed to a bifurcated settlement that, subject to court approval, permanently enjoins him from further violations and reserves monetary determinations for a later date.
