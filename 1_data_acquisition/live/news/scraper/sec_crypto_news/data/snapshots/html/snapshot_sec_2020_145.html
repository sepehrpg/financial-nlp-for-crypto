SEC Charges Issuer, CEO, and Lobbyist With Defrauding Investors in AML BitCoin

For Immediate Release

2020-145

Washington D.C., 2020-06-25 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission charged NAC Foundation, its CEO Marcus Andrade, and lobbyist Jack Abramoff with conducting a fraudulent, unregistered offering of AML BitCoin, described as a new and improved version of bitcoin.

The SEC alleges NAC raised at least $5.6 million from more than 2,400 investors and portrayed AML BitCoin as superior to the original bitcoin with anti-money laundering and theft-resistant technology.

The complaints allege the touted capabilities did not exist, government-use claims were false, and approximately $1.1 million was diverted for personal use.

The SEC seeks injunctions, disgorgement, civil penalties, and restrictions on future offerings and public-company service.
