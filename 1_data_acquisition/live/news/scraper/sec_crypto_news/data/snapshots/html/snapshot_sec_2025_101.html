SEC Permits In-Kind Creations and Redemptions for Crypto ETPs

For Immediate Release

2025-101

Washington D.C., 2025-07-29 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission today voted to approve orders to permit in-kind creations and redemptions by authorized participants for crypto asset exchange-traded product shares.

The orders reflect a departure from recently approved spot bitcoin and ether ETPs, which were limited to creations and redemptions on an in-cash basis. With the approvals, bitcoin and ether ETPs will be permitted to create and redeem shares on an in-kind basis.

SEC Chairman Paul S. Atkins said the orders would make the products less costly and more efficient and were part of developing a fit-for-purpose framework for crypto asset markets.

The Commission also approved exchange applications involving an ETP holding mixed spot bitcoin and spot ether, options on certain spot bitcoin ETPs, FLEX options on shares of certain BTC-based ETPs, and increased position limits for listed options on certain BTC ETPs.
