SEC Charges Former Bitcoin-Denominated Exchange and Operator With Fraud

For Immediate Release

2018-23

Washington D.C., 2018-02-21 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission charged a former bitcoin-denominated platform and its operator with operating an unregistered securities exchange and defrauding users.

The SEC alleges BitFunder and founder Jon E. Montroll misappropriated users’ bitcoins and failed to disclose a cyberattack that resulted in the theft of more than 6,000 bitcoins.

The complaint also alleges Montroll sold unregistered securities and misappropriated investment funds. The SEC seeks injunctions, disgorgement plus interest, and penalties.
