SEC Charges Creator of Global Crypto Ponzi Scheme and Three US Promoters in Connection with $295 Million Fraud

Trade Coin Club raised money from more than 100,000 investors worldwide

For Immediate Release

2022-201

Washington D.C., 2022-11-04 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission announced charges against Douver Torres Braga, Joff Paradise, Keleionalani Akana Taylor, and Jonathan Tetreault for roles in Trade Coin Club, a fraudulent crypto Ponzi scheme that raised more than 82,000 bitcoin, valued at $295 million at the time, from more than 100,000 investors.

Trade Coin Club operated from 2016 through 2018 and promised profits from a purported crypto asset trading bot. The SEC alleges promoters made false representations about millions of microtransactions and minimum daily returns.

Investor withdrawals allegedly came entirely from deposits by other investors. Braga personally received at least 8,396 bitcoin, while other promoters received additional bitcoin amounts.

The complaints allege violations of antifraud, securities registration, and broker-dealer registration provisions and seek injunctive relief, disgorgement, and civil penalties.
