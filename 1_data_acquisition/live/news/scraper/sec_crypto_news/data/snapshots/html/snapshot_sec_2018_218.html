SEC Charges Bitcoin-Funded Securities Dealer and CEO

For Immediate Release

2018-218

Washington D.C., 2018-09-27 —

The Securities and Exchange Commission filed charges against an international securities dealer and its CEO for alleged securities-law violations involving security-based swaps funded with bitcoins.

Investors could open accounts with minimal information and could only fund accounts using bitcoins. The SEC alleges the platform failed to meet registration, investor eligibility, and exchange requirements.

The complaint seeks permanent injunctions, disgorgement plus interest, and penalties.
